# Draft LinkedIn Post: Orqaly × AxWise Migration & Pivot

What if AI agents acted more like... humans? At least, what if they tried to do so?

For the past year at AxWise, we have been obsessed with building the ultimate human-behavioral simulation engine. But we hit a massive adoption chasm: it is incredibly hard for teams to use synthetic personas and integrate them into their active workflows and pipelines—especially when they don't know exactly how they work under the hood.

As we see, most AI platforms today are toys. One-shot prompts lose their constraints after a few turns. Brittle web interfaces force operators to jump out of their existing developer environments. And let's be honest, the entire legacy 'Design Thinking & customer research interview' market is incredibly hard to convert and a bit too small for our ambition.

So, we decided to make a massive, transparent shift.

Following the departure of my co-founder, Jadon Wu, who has decided to step down, we took a hard look at the product direction. 

Rather than scaling back, we decided to pivot and merge AxWise with Orqaly https://orqaly.com to launch a singular, open-source engine that turns a simple sentence into a working business: adding an agentic and council monitoring layer for modern operations.

Here is what we did, what is being removed, and how we are building the future of sovereign, agentic workforces:

🛑 **We are completely removing the legacy scoping, simulation, and interview analysis interface.** While great for academic modeling, it simply didn't ground to real-world, high-throughput company operations.

Instead, we pivoted, merging AxWise’s behavioral simulation engine directly into Orqaly’s Agentic Orchestrator to create a single, unified engine that serves two critical user needs:

1️⃣ **Intent Ingestion & Market Testing:** Whether it's a quick weather check, email connection, chat ingestion, or something bigger like deep business research—we ingest your company rules, context, and latest updates to simulate product demand. The engine spins up verified customer personas in seconds to quickly identify what problem we want to serve and for whom.

2️⃣ **Dynamic Agent Formulation:** Then, we identify and generate the perfect internal agents—who we recruit to solve your problem directly inside your Claude Code, Cursor, or whatever IDE/vibecoding tool you are using. The engine automatically generates highly specialized engineering personas (e.g., AI Orchestration Developers, Security Sandboxing Engineers, or MarTech Experts) needed to bring that specific product or campaign to life.

3️⃣ **Grounded Persona DNA:** We created OCEAN-grounded profiles for both stakeholders (users) and agents (executors), ensuring high psychological variance, behavioral reliability, and highly accurate, multi-party descriptions.

4️⃣ **Sub-Millisecond Semantic Retrieval:** Digital twins are now strictly grounded in your private company partitions (SOPs, guides, directories) using pgvector on Postgres, with an in-memory python-vector fallback on SQLite for local development.

5️⃣ **Secure Runtime Impersonation:** Every operational request (via integrated channels like Slack or Cursor MCP) must pass through our integrated Policy Gateway (RBAC) before execution, maintaining absolute data sovereignty and a strict budget cap in USD, EUR, or tokens.

Why this is a game-changer for anyone from casual users to engineering teams: No more pasting long documents into ChatGPT or Claude. You can register the Orqaly server in your local Model Context Protocol (MCP) config, bring your own API key (BYOK), and let grounded, persona-driven agents execute complex tasks directly inside your IDE without context drift.
