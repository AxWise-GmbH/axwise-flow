# Unpublished Legal Center drafts

This directory is retained for review, not as approved public policy. The website
design release does not route to `LegalCenter`, import its document loader, or
publish `security.txt.draft`. Existing `/privacy`, `/terms` and `/cookies` notices
remain unchanged. They are placeholders, not a completed legal setup.

Before a separate publication change, resolve the internal review notes, company
identity and governing law, confirm contact mailboxes and operational commitments,
and reconcile the privacy/provider disclosures with the actual optional
JEV/Typesafe data flow. Obtain owner and appropriate legal review. Remove internal
review notes from any public document assets and confirm the deployed build.

The component tests exercise these drafts in isolation; passing them does not
authorize their publication or make their policy statements accurate.
