# Latvian Ecommerce Market Overview

*Prepared: 2026-07-04 | Role: Business Development Manager*

---

## 1. Market Size & Growth

Latvia's ecommerce market was valued at **USD 789 million** in 2025, with **77% of internet users shopping online** and mobile commerce accounting for **58% of all transaction value** [16]. A broader definition (including B2B) places the market at **USD 3.01 billion in 2024**, projected to reach **USD 6.31 billion by 2032** at a **CAGR of 9.7%** [3].

Revenue is expected to grow at a CAGR of **8.81% (2024-2029)**, reaching a projected volume of **USD 1.12 billion** by 2029 ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/outlook/emo/ecommerce/latvia)). The broader Baltic States combined online sales revenue was estimated at **USD 3.7 billion in 2023** [21].

## 3. Consumer Demographics & Behavior

- **Online shopper penetration:** 77% of internet users shop online; roughly **7 in 10 Latvian residents** have purchased goods/services online [16] ([Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php?title=E-commerce_statistics_for_individuals)).
- **Most active age group:** 25-34 years old; penetration is slightly higher among women [18].
- **Regional disparity:** Riga region accounts for 65.8% of Latvia's GDP with ~75% online shopping penetration. Latgale (per-capita income 2.6x lower) has 38-45% penetration but is the **fastest-growing region** [16].
- **Top product categories purchased online:** Clothing, shoes, and accessories lead at **26% of Latvians** purchasing in a three-month period ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/topics/11331/e-commerce-in-latvia/)).
- **Price sensitivity:** Latvian shoppers are highly price-conscious, with Salidzini.lv's popularity as a comparison tool reflecting this behavior [16].
- **Conservative traits:** Significant conservatism among older and lower-education e-shoppers, but the number of low-income e-shoppers is growing rapidly [17].

## 5. Logistics Infrastructure

### Delivery Preferences

**Parcel terminals are the #1 delivery method** in the Baltics. The majority of respondents prefer to receive orders in parcel terminals [12]. **87% of Latvians** reported receiving at least one parcel last year [14].

### Parcel Locker Networks (Pan-Baltic, late 2025)

| Operator | Locker Count (Pan-Baltic) | Latvia Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Omniva** | ~1,250 | Market leader; expanding into smaller populated areas beyond Riga [25] |
| **Unisend** | ~1,150 | Strong Pan-Baltic presence [25] |
| **DPD** | ~1,090 | Major logistics player [25] |
| **Venipak** | ~1,000 | Growing 38% in Latvia; planning 1,500 lockers within two years [25] |
| **Latvijas Pasts** | 420+ (Latvia only) | State-owned postal service; widest Latvia-specific network; building home parcel lockers [28][29] |

**Latvia has 14.5 out-of-home pickup points per 10,000 inhabitants** [27]. Six companies operate parcel networks in Latvia, and **13.3 million parcels** were sent and received [26].

### Key Logistics Characteristics

- **Next-business-day delivery** standard within Latvia via parcel lockers [28]
- **7-day parcel hold** at locker stations [28]
- Cross-border logistics supported by multiple providers; UK-Latvia corridors well-served [9]
- Omniva is the most popular parcel terminal provider in Latvia and Estonia [12]

## 6. Key Takeaways for Market Entry

1. **Growing market with room to run** - 9.7% CAGR through 2032, with mobile commerce at 58% and rising [3][16]
2. **Price comparison is king** - Salidzini.lv with 1.3M monthly users means competitive pricing is essential for visibility
3. **Parcel lockers are the default** - Any ecommerce operation must integrate with Omniva, DPD, and Venipak locker APIs
4. **Card payments dominate** - Visa/Mastercard integration is non-negotiable; add bank transfers for conversion uplift
5. **Riga is the primary market** - 65.8% of GDP, but Latgale is the fastest-growing region for ecommerce adoption [16]
6. **ss.lv for C2C/testing** - Low barrier to entry; ideal for testing product-market fit before investing in a standalone store
7. **Cross-border competition** - Latvian consumers buy from amazon.de and international sites, so pricing must be competitive
