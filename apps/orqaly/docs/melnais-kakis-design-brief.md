## 1. Personas

### Persona 1: Liga, 32 - The Devoted Cat Mom (Riga)

**Demographics:** Female, 32, lives in a 2-room apartment in Riga's Centrs district. Works as a project manager at an IT company. Net monthly income EUR 1,800. Single, one black domestic shorthair named Muris.

**Behavior:** Shops online weekly - uses Rimi e-veikals for groceries, Zara for clothing, Amazon.de for specialty items. 77% of Latvian internet users shop online [Statista/WebWorks 2025], and Liga is firmly in that majority. Mobile-first - 58% of Latvia's e-commerce transactions happen on mobile devices [WebWorks Latvia E-commerce Report 2025]. She scrolls Instagram and TikTok daily, follows cat accounts, and screenshots products she wants to try.

**Pet food journey:** Currently buys Brit Care (EUR 5-8/kg) from PetShop.lv because it's "good enough" but knows it's not the best. Has tried Royal Canin samples from her vet but finds it too clinical and expensive at EUR 9.50-11.33/kg [PetShop.lv pricing]. Reads ingredient labels. Wants grain-free, high-protein options but doesn't want to pay Orijen prices (EUR 17.95/kg) [PetShop.lv].

**Pain points:** No Latvian brand speaks to her identity. International brands feel generic - same packaging, same messaging across 30 countries. She wants a brand that feels personal, local, and worth posting about.

**Trigger to buy:** Strong visual identity she can share on social media. Subscription convenience with parcel locker delivery (Omniva, DPD). A price point in the EUR 7-10/kg range that feels premium without being absurd.

---

### Persona 2: Martins, 38 - The Practical Family Man (Jelgava)

**Demographics:** Male, 38, married with two children (ages 4 and 7). Lives in a house in Jelgava (40km from Riga). Works as an electrical engineer. Household net income EUR 3,200. Two cats - a tabby and a black-and-white domestic.

**Behavior:** Shops online less frequently than Liga but is comfortable with it - mostly uses parcel lockers (Omniva) for convenience. Latvia's online-buyer ratio reached 65% of the population in 2024, up from 51% in 2021 [Statista]. Martins upgraded from cash to card payments and now uses Montonio bank links for faster checkout. He researches purchases on his laptop before buying on his phone.

**Pet food journey:** Buys whatever his wife puts on the shopping list, usually Purina ONE or Pro Plan from Maxima (EUR 4-8/kg). Aware that his older cat has started having digestive issues. His veterinarian suggested a higher-quality food but didn't specify a brand. He's open to switching if someone makes the decision easy for him.

**Pain points:** Overwhelmed by options when he visits PetShop.lv - too many brands, no clear guidance on what's actually better. Doesn't trust marketing claims. Wants clear nutritional facts and vet endorsement.

**Trigger to buy:** Simple product comparison. Vet-approved claims with specific nutritional data. A subscription that auto-delivers so he doesn't have to think about it. Free shipping on orders over EUR 30.

---

### Persona 3: Elina, 27 - The Eco-Conscious Young Professional (Riga)

**Demographics:** Female, 27, shares a flat in Riga's Agenskalns neighborhood with a roommate. Works in marketing at a creative agency. Net monthly income EUR 1,400. One rescue cat, a black Maine Coon mix named Nakts ("Night").

**Behavior:** Digital native. Orders everything online. Active on Instagram, Pinterest, and Latvian Facebook groups for cat owners (Kakis Mans Draugs - "Cat is My Friend" has 15,000+ members). Latvia's Care Products e-commerce segment accounts for 9% of total online retail [ECDB]. She values brands with a story, sustainable packaging, and transparent sourcing.

**Pet food journey:** Has cycled through Josera (EUR 7-10/kg), Farmina N&D (EUR 11.93/kg), and raw food from Bio Raw Pet Food Latvia. Willing to pay premium but wants to know exactly where the food comes from and how it's made. The "black cat mystique" angle aligns perfectly with her own cat and her aesthetic sensibility.

**Pain points:** Latvian pet food brands have zero personality. Sustainability claims are vague. She wants a brand that's transparent about sourcing, uses recyclable packaging, and has a visual identity worth supporting. In Latvian folklore, black cats are the spirit of Rungis, the harvest god - a symbol of prosperity and abundance, not bad luck [Wikipedia / Baltic mythology sources]. She knows this and loves it.

**Trigger to buy:** A brand story that connects to Latvian heritage. Instagram-worthy packaging. Transparent ingredient sourcing from named EU manufacturers. Community membership (not just a transaction).

## 2. 7-Block Selling Architecture

### Block 1: HERO

**Goal:** Stop the scroll. Establish premium positioning and black cat identity in 3 seconds.

**English headline:** "Your cat deserves food worthy of a legend."
**Latvian headline:** "Tavs kakis ir pelnijis baribu, kas cieniga legendai."

**English sub:** "Melnais Kakis - premium cat food rooted in Latvian tradition. Grain-free. High-protein. Delivered to your door."
**Latvian sub:** "Melnais Kakis - augstaklas kaku bariba, kas saknojas latviesu tradicijas. Bez graudaugiem. Ar augstu olbaltumvielu saturu. Piegade lidz jusu durvim."

**Visual:** Full-width dark background. A black cat with golden eyes sits beside a matte-black food bowl with gold trim. Moody, low-key lighting with warm amber highlights. The Melnais Kakis logo (a stylized black cat silhouette with a gold eye) sits top-left.

**CTA:** "Pieteikties agrinai pieejai" / "Get early access" - email capture form with a single input field and amber button.

---

### Block 2: PROBLEM

**Goal:** Name the pain so visitors feel understood.

**English headline:** "Premium cat food in Latvia shouldn't mean choosing between a German label you can't read and a supermarket bag you don't trust."
**Latvian headline:** "Augstaklas kaku baribai Latvija nevajadzetu nozīmet izveli starp vacu etiketi, ko nevarat izlasit, un lielveikala maisinu, kuram neuzticaties."

**Content (3 pain points):**
1. **Generic imports, no local identity** - "Every premium brand on PetShop.lv was designed for someone in Munich or Milan. None of them were made for Latvian cats or Latvian cat owners."
2. **Price confusion** - "EUR 2/kg or EUR 18/kg? The gap between supermarket food and ultra-premium is a canyon. You need something in between - quality you can verify, at a price that makes sense."
3. **No transparency** - "What's actually in the bag? Most brands bury their ingredient sourcing behind marketing language. You deserve to know where your cat's food comes from."

**Visual:** Split layout - left side shows a cluttered supermarket shelf (generic, overwhelming). Right side fades to clean, dark space with the Melnais Kakis bag sitting alone, elevated.

---

### Block 3: SOLUTION

**Goal:** Position Melnais Kakis as the answer - local, transparent, premium but not pretentious.

**English headline:** "Food made for Latvian cats. By people who actually live here."
**Latvian headline:** "Bariba, kas radita latviesu kakiem. No cilvekiem, kas te dzivo."

**Content:**
- Sourced from EU-certified manufacturers (VAFO Group, Czech Republic and United Petfood, Belgium) with full traceability [Business Plan - Supplier Shortlist]
- Grain-free, high-protein formulas (minimum 40% animal protein)
- Latvian-language labeling, Latvian customer support, Latvian brand identity
- Delivered via Omniva parcel lockers (EUR 2.49), DPD (EUR 3.50), or Venipak (EUR 2.89) - the networks you already use [Business Plan - Operations]
- EUR 7.49-9.99/kg - positioned above mass-market, below ultra-premium

**Visual:** Three cards on dark background. Card 1: EU flag + factory icon ("EU-certified production"). Card 2: Ingredient list close-up ("Full transparency"). Card 3: Omniva parcel locker photo ("Free delivery over EUR 35").

### Block 5: FEATURES

**Goal:** Detail the three product pillars with specifics, not vague claims.

**Feature 1: Natural Ingredients**
- **EN:** "Real meat first. Always."
- **LV:** "Ista gala vienmer ir pirma."
- Detail: Minimum 40% animal protein from named sources (chicken, turkey, salmon). No corn, wheat, or soy fillers. No artificial colors or preservatives. Sourced from EU-approved suppliers operating under Regulation (EC) No 183/2005 feed hygiene standards [Research Brief - EU Regulations].

**Feature 2: Vet-Approved Nutrition**
- **EN:** "Formulated with veterinary nutritionists. Not a marketing team."
- **LV:** "Izstradaja veterinarie uztura specialisti. Ne marketinga komanda."
- Detail: Complete and balanced nutrition meeting FEDIAF nutritional guidelines. Tailored formulas for indoor cats, sterilized cats, and kittens. Added taurine, omega-3/6 fatty acids, and prebiotics for digestive health.

**Feature 3: The Black Cat Collection**
- **EN:** "Packaging you'll want to keep on the counter."
- **LV:** "Iepakojums, ko gribēsiet atstat uz letes."
- Detail: Matte-black pouches and tins with gold foil accents. Each product features original black cat illustrations inspired by Latvian folk art. The black cat in Latvian folklore is the spirit of Rungis, the harvest god - a symbol of prosperity, not superstition [Latvian mythology / Baltic folklore sources]. Recyclable packaging with clear recycling instructions in Latvian.

### Block 7: FAQ + FOOTER

**Goal:** Remove final objections, provide legal compliance, and close.

**FAQ (5 questions):**

**Q1: Ka notiek piegade? / How does shipping work?**
A: We deliver across Latvia via Omniva parcel lockers (EUR 2.49), DPD courier (EUR 3.50), and Venipak (EUR 2.89). Orders over EUR 35 ship free. Delivery takes 1-3 business days. We also ship to Estonia and Lithuania.

**Q2: Kadas ir sastavdalas? / What are the ingredients?**
A: Every product lists full ingredients on the packaging and on our product pages. Our dry food starts with named animal protein (minimum 40%) - chicken, turkey, or salmon. No corn, wheat, soy, artificial colors, or preservatives. All ingredients meet EU feed safety standards under Regulation (EC) No 183/2005.

**Q3: Vai varu atgriezt produktu? / Can I return the product?**
A: Yes. If your cat doesn't love it, contact us within 30 days for a full refund. Unopened products can be returned at no cost. We comply with Latvia's Consumer Rights Protection Law (Pateretaju tiesibu aizsardzibas likums) for 14-day withdrawal rights on distance sales.

**Q4: Ka darbojas abonements? / How does the subscription work?**
A: Choose your product and delivery frequency (every 2, 4, or 6 weeks). We charge your card automatically and ship to your preferred Omniva, DPD, or Venipak pickup point. Skip, pause, or cancel anytime - no lock-in contracts. Manage everything from your account dashboard.

**Q5: Vai bariba ir piemērota visiem kakiem? / Is the food suitable for all cats?**
A: Our Standard formula is designed for healthy adult cats (1-7 years). We also offer formulas for kittens (up to 12 months), sterilized cats, indoor cats, and senior cats (7+). If your cat has specific medical dietary needs, consult your veterinarian first.

## 4. Design System

### CSS Custom Properties (literal :root block - copy verbatim into HTML)

```css
:root {
  --color-primary:    #1E293B;  /* Dark navy - hero background, nav, footer, dark section anchors */
  --color-secondary:  #475569;  /* Slate - card backgrounds, hover states, secondary containers */
  --color-accent:     #F59E0B;  /* Amber gold - CTAs, highlights, the cat's eye, price tags, focus borders */
  --color-background: #FAFAF9;  /* Warm off-white - light section backgrounds, FAQ surface, page base */
  --color-text:       #0F172A;  /* Near-black ink - body text on light backgrounds, headlines on light sections */
  --color-border:     #E2E8F0;  /* Cool light grey - card borders, dividers, input borders at rest */
}
```
