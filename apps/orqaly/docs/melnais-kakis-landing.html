Frequently Asked Questions

Biezi uzdotie jautajumi

Ka notiek piegade? / How does shipping work? expand_more

We deliver across Latvia via Omniva parcel lockers (EUR 2.49), DPD courier (EUR 3.50), and Venipak (EUR 2.89). Orders over EUR 35 ship free. Delivery takes 1-3 business days. We also ship to Estonia and Lithuania.

Kadas ir sastavdalas? / What are the ingredients? expand_more

Every product lists full ingredients on the packaging and on our product pages. Our dry food starts with named animal protein (minimum 40%) - chicken, turkey, or salmon. No corn, wheat, soy, artificial colors, or preservatives. All ingredients meet EU feed safety standards under Regulation (EC) No 183/2005.

Vai varu atgriezt produktu? / Can I return the product? expand_more

Yes. If your cat doesn't love it, contact us within 30 days for a full refund. Unopened products can be returned at no cost. We comply with Latvia's Consumer Rights Protection Law (Pateretaju tiesibu aizsardzibas likums) for 14-day withdrawal rights on distance sales.

Ka darbojas abonements? / How does the subscription work? expand_more

Choose your product and delivery frequency (every 2, 4, or 6 weeks). We charge your card automatically and ship to your preferred Omniva, DPD, or Venipak pickup point. Skip, pause, or cancel anytime - no lock-in contracts. Manage everything from your account dashboard.

Vai bariba ir piemErota visiem kakiem? / Is the food suitable for all cats? expand_more

Our Standard formula is designed for healthy adult cats (1-7 years). We also offer formulas for kittens (up to 12 months), sterilized cats, indoor cats, and senior cats (7+). If your cat has specific medical dietary needs, consult your veterinarian first.
