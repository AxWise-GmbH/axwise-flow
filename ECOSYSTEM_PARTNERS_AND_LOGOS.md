## 5. Website Usage Guidelines

1. **Active Program Badges:** On `axwise.de`, only active accelerator & incubator partners (**Constructor Start Accelerator** and **Imaguru Startup Hub**) are featured in the accelerator header bar.
2. **Anonymous Community Marquee:** In the scrolling social proof marquee, company logos appear without individual developer names to maintain privacy.
3. **Historical Record:** Former affiliations like AI Nation remain cataloged internally for due diligence and investor histories but are omitted from public marketing banners.
