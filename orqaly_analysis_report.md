# Behavioral Patterns

### 1. Capped Multi-Agent Orchestration

**Category:** Workflow

Users follow a four-stage execution pipeline: defining a budget-capped goal, allowing a multi-agent council to decompose the task, executing in isolated containers, and reviewing outputs via an audit queue.

**Impact:** Prevents "black box" failures and cost overruns by modularizing tasks and enforcing resource limits before and during execution.

### 2. Policy-Gated Information Retrieval

**Category:** Decision Process

The system automatically mediates data requests by passing queries through a policy engine that checks the requester's role-based access control (RBAC) permissions before allowing a 'digital twin' to release information.

**Impact:** Ensures that automated agents do not become vectors for data leaks, maintaining organizational security even when employees are OOO.

### 3. Synthetic Persona Persona-Matching

**Category:** Role-Specific Behavior

Digital twins adopt specific linguistic styles and analytical frameworks—such as formal financial modeling or collaborative design feedback—to mirror the professional identity of the human they represent.

**Impact:** Increases trust and seamlessness in communication, as stakeholders interact with a persona that behaves predictably and professionally like their human counterpart.

### 4. Continuous Audit Verification

**Category:** Trust Verification

Users insist on a 'second opinion' and verifiable execution trails to overcome the fragility and 'black box' nature of traditional one-shot AI prompts.

**Impact:** Moves AI from a 'toy' status to an enterprise-grade tool by providing human operators with the evidence needed to trust autonomous results.

### 5. Cross-Platform Knowledge Interception

**Category:** Information Seeking

Users interact with digital twins across multiple communication channels (Slack, WhatsApp, Messenger) to retrieve specific data points from private company partitions.

**Impact:** Reduces information silos and prevents work bottlenecks when key personnel are unavailable or in different time zones.
